# Location information side panel

Selecting a map destination opens a right-side profile before its existing full detail page. The panel stays mounted when another destination is selected; the map flight continues while only the profile's photograph and text transition. The map canvas retains its dimensions. Names, coordinates, full-detail routes and QR utilities remain unchanged.

The latest user brief replaces the centered exhibition window with an editorial side panel. The implementation retains the existing charcoal, warm gold, Noto Sans and Lucide system. Design dials remain DESIGN_VARIANCE 5, MOTION_INTENSITY 6 and VISUAL_DENSITY 5. No new packages or images were downloaded.

Large desktops use `clamp(420px, 31vw, 560px)` with 24 px insets inside the map viewport, below the global header. The remaining map occupies about 70% of a 1920 px screen. Laptops use a 440 px panel and 16 px insets; portrait tablets use approximately 52vw. Zoom tools, bearing controls and the destination dock occupy the available map area. On narrow tablets, automatic touring and overview actions use a separate row beneath the basemap selector. Collapsing the destination dock retains its compact width.

The reading order is compact QR/close header, category and location title, address, photograph and thumbnails, semantic tabs, paginated information and an anchored outline detail action. Title, image, tabs and action remain in one viewport. The content area does not scroll vertically. `QuickLocationReader.tsx` measures the actual text at its current width and divides long descriptions into pages without dropping words or headings. Overview, Highlights and Visiting have independent page positions. A destination change starts at Overview. Portrait phones use a nearly full-screen sheet; landscape phones place images and information beside each other. Short screens compact spacing and thumbnails. Full photograph credits remain available in the image title when the caption row is omitted.

`locationQuickContent.ts` contains Vietnamese/English introductions and visit guidance for all seven destinations. Existing site chapters supply full highlight descriptions. Museum copy uses the reviewed detail-page material: Khmer architecture, community objects and performing arts. The 1992–1997 formation period distinguishes construction, inauguration and visitor-opening accounts. No opening hours, prices, bookable performances or current services are invented. Museum photographs retain their 2023 VietNamNet captions and credit. Generic Unsplash illustrations and the Ao Ba Om image previously labelled as Chua Hang are excluded; Hang Pagoda has one documented image in this panel.

`QuickLocationGallery.tsx` supplies previous/next arrows, three-image thumbnail galleries where available, photo counter, caption/credit, keyboard arrows and horizontal touch swipes. Images crossfade for 650 ms after loading. Loading and failed-photo states retain the information and detail action. Photo selection survives language changes and returning from full details. A single-photo destination does not reserve an empty thumbnail row.

`useLocationQuickMotion.ts` opens the panel from the right using a 720 ms translation, opacity and subtle scale transition. Another destination retains the same shell: the old image fades left, the replacement fades in from the right, the title masks out/in, and supporting content follows a short stagger. Temporary snapshots are decorative, inert and removed after the transition. The selected map marker has a warmer halo and a temporary pulse. Exit travels right for 280 ms. Tabs move one gold indicator and animate only the information area; reading-page changes animate only their text. Gallery camera drift, native pointer parallax, thumbnail progress, control feedback and the detail button's gold sweep preserve the richer motion.

The map shade uses static 1 px blur and opacity, without animating blur or resizing the canvas. Parallax is suspended during opening, destination replacement and the detail handoff. Native animations, frames, observers, listeners and decorative nodes are disposed on close, replacement, rapid interaction and StrictMode remount. System and host reduced-motion preferences cancel active transitions, including a destination change already in progress, and leave content immediately usable.

Museum preparation starts after the panel entrance settles or earlier on detail intent. Its asynchronous callback verifies that the persistent panel still belongs to the museum before preparing a full page. The existing photograph-to-full-page bridge retains its crop geometry and clears pointer translation. Full details preserve deferred chapter loading, paused covered-map work, visited sections, QR/artifact/panorama dialogs and return-focus behavior.

`locationQuickView.css` contains the responsive panel and motion styles; existing utility dialogs retain `locationQuickUtility.css`. Focused Chrome/CDP commands are `node tests/museo-detail-test.cjs --quick-window-only`, `--quick-window-motion-only`, `--quick-view-only` and `--opening-only`. Functional checks compare every word, heading and metadata block across all reader pages for seven locations, both languages and ten viewport sizes: 1920×1080, 1440×960, 1366×768, 1024×768, 768×960, 640×400, 390×844, 320×568, 844×390 and 667×375. Additional checks cover map controls and destination navigation, retained panel identity, gallery interaction, live motion cancellation, image failure/recovery, nested utilities and full-page opening. No new performance score is claimed.

Production validation passed 31 focused functional checks covering 1,085 reading-page combinations, 14 normal-motion checks, 211 host integration checks and 13 museum-opening lifecycle checks. The suites reported no missing local assets or browser exceptions. Visual review covered desktop, tablet, portrait phones and landscape phones. Production build, TypeScript and whitespace checks passed. The build retains its existing warning about large bundled chunks.
